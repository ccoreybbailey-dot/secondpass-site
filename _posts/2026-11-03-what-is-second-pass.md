---
title: "What is Second Pass, and how does it work?"
date: 2026-11-03
description: "Why I built Second Pass, an iPhone app that plays your own thoughts back to you, in your own voice, at a moment you didn't pick."
---
For the past few months I've been working on an app called Second Pass. The idea is pretty simple. You
speak into it like you would any normal voice memo app, to uncover valuable insight you may already
possess from books, videos, or just life experience. Stuff you've learned and want to remember. You can
also brain dump and choose what comes back afterwards. The important thing is that it's in your own
voice. It's you giving yourself wisdom.

The idea came to me for two reasons. The first is that I had started journaling out loud, through voice
memos and sometimes video recordings. The second is that one day I was sitting around doom scrolling my
life away when a video said something along the lines of "go do something with your life." It was
nothing groundbreaking, but it did snap me out of the trance, and I realized we already have the
insight. We just need to be reminded of it.

Journaling is useful, but it can be a kind of write it down and forget it. I think the act of writing
something down, or even recording it, is still valuable. Second Pass lets you take a second look at what
you've already said, at a time when you might not be thinking about it. You choose the passages
yourself, cutting through the noise of a brain dump, and what you chose comes back to you at a random
time.

There's a feature called Listening. When it's on and your headphones are in, it will interrupt you at
random with one of your passages, in your own voice. There's also a widget, so when you pick up your
phone, or it's sitting in StandBy, you see one of your passages written out.

The app really leaves it up to you. There's no login and no push notifications. It's just there when you
want some insight from yourself. It's for people who are self-reflective and introspective. Nothing in
it decides what you should hear. You choose what comes back.

I know for myself I'm always trying to keep my mind clear and focused, and I often get overwhelmed and
off track in the process. Second Pass is there for those moments.
